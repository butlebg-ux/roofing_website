Industrial & Commercial Roofing FAQs

What roofing systems do you recommend for large distribution centers?

For large-footprint logistics facilities, mechanically attached or fully adhered TPO and EPDM single-ply membranes offer optimal energy efficiency, UV reflection, and puncture resistance.

Are you certified for multi-million dollar commercial projects?

Yes, Premier maintains full multi-million dollar bonding capacity and comprehensive general liability and workers' compensation coverage tailored for enterprise-level complexes.
